

Actually, *sell short*. Financial news never tell you, but it's true: you never, ever make money buying.

You can only make money when you sell. The so-called "investing" is buying (losing money) with the expectation of making a profit in the future. So "investing" is spending money on some kind of a remote expectation. If people were honest with themselves they'd realize it's the opposite of what they should be doing. They should sell some kind of a distant expectation, and keep the money. In each of my strategies I'm expecting to make money with the short leg.

I also proudly don't show the squiggly line. The 1-day, 1-month, etc price history of an asset. Looking at it is depressing, and addictive. When it's in your favor you are glued to it, unable to focus on anything else. When it goes against you, you are even more glued to it, unable to focus on anything else. It's gambling, it's addictive. So I only show decision-making numbers. Automatic decisions will be by these numbers. No squiggly line!
